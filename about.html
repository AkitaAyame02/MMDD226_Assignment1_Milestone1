Pakwan Island Discoveries is a website run by Matsuda Souta (Souta), Matsuda Kanro (Admin K), and Andrew Gauthier-Desjardins (AGD).

It's clear to us that no matter what, Rooster Island had existed at some point in time. However, one day, Rooster Island was destroyed. Along with it, its history. It was almost like Rooster Island had disappeared forever. Meanwhile, a new island seemingly appeared out of nowhere. This is Pakwan Island, where we are today. While the world may have forgotten Rooster Island, it will always find a way to be remembered. After all, without it, Pakwan Island would likely have never existed. But who will piece the remnants of the forgotten past? Who shall put things in the right order? Who would continue unearthing the relics?

Who will tell Rooster Island's story?

Finally, that's where we come in. Our team is dedicated to unearthing the history of both Rooster and Pakwan, bit by bit. Whether that's by finding cassette tapes, connecting to other people, or simply theorizing. No matter what, we are determined to continue looking for these questions, and get steps closer to answering them. Hopefully, we will finally have a full story of how and why Rooster Island was destroyed, and how Pakwan Island came to be.

This is a fansite and is not associated with any of the featured franchises (other than my OCs) in any way. Resident pictures drawn by me.
