Update about Andrew

Co-written by Admin K and Souta

Okay, it feels like that we need to have an update about one of our admins.

No, AGD is not dead. He is very much alive. However, he hasn't reached out to either of us. Yeah, this is despite the fact that we're live on the same tiny-ish island. Theoretically, we shouldn't be able to avoid him. But in practice, a "tiny" island is still very large. He continously slips from either of us.

When he first started avoiding us, he was solving a pretty major problem. It was much more important than figuring out what happened to Rooster Island. It was understandable. If it continued, we don't think that this island would be habitable. Andrew said that it reminded him of what happened in Rooster Island. The parallels are almost eerie.

This problem still isn't solved. However, it's much less prevalent than before, and it's not spreading anymore. We thought that with that, Andrew would come back to our team! Y'know, continuing the investigation and all? However, he still hasn't come back to us.

It's not like he has to constantly be with us every day. He could just ask us "hey, have you found something new" once in a while, and that'd be enough for the two of us, really. But he's gone no-contact. We've asked his housemates if we could talk to him, and they have. But then we see him, and he claims that he's busy with something else and shooos us away.

Obviously, we were gonna be upset. It wasn't about the investigation anymore. One of our first friends is pushing us away without an explaination. Kanro was much more upset. She had an argument with Andrew as a result. We did end up patching things up, but even now, we still feel a tension between us. We think that that tension's making Andy avoid us even more. It's now more convenient.

But we're not gonna let that fact bring us down. We'll still update y'all about what we discovered and more! We just thought we'd let you know why activity has slowed down a little.

This is weird...

Written by Souta

Something strange happened about two weeks ago. I was in Tomoria, having some food. One of the residents ran up to me and asked me if I was a pediatrician.

I told them that I wasn't a pediatrician (yet), but that I was studying pediatrics. They said "good enough" and practically dragged me out of the restaurant and onto the beach.

A bunch of people were surrounding something, all with worried looks on their faces. One of them was performing CPR. I approached the small crowd, and they all seemed relieved that I was there. It was a child. She looked no older than six.

I immediately went on autopilot. I yelled at someone to call the emergency number. I cycled between CPR and rescue breaths for so long. I don't recall how long I've done this. Doesn't matter though. After a while, she threw up on me, clinging onto my shirt for support. I started laughing in relief. I didn't care that my white shirt was stained. She was alive. Hugh Morris pushed through the crowd and hugged her, tears in his eyes. Bay trailed behind him, watching her father hug this child like there was no tomorrow.

I've heard Bay mention that she had a younger sister a few times before. She mentioned that she barely remembered her, only that she had purple hair like her dad. Based on Hugh's reaction upon seeing this child and Bay's description, I assume that this child is Bay's younger sister.

Alongside her, there were a bunch of unknown citrus flowers floating in the water. They looked similar to the flowers in the casette tapes. Or at least, what they were described as.

We learned that her name was Gardenia White. Kanro asked Hugh why both of his children didn't take his surname. He looked nervous and told us that it was a story for another day.

A few days later, Gardenia was walking out and about on the island. Unusually, she had a pet spirit alongside her. I thought it was a ghost. They're often used as synonyms. But Kanro said that it wasn't. Andrew suggested that it might me a spirit in the sense of forest spirits. A living being that was born incorporeal, rather than a being who died and became incorporeal. That's why Kanro couldn't detect it. At least, that's what I think. Andrew speaks in riddles at times, and this was no exception. I'd say curse those with magic, but my sister has it and she's just as confused as me, so...

Curse people who were born with magic.

Anyways, without a doubt, Gardenia's connected to Rooster Island. She came along with the flowers.

Ghosts

Written by Admin K

The weirdest thing happened today.

I woke up so early, like 3AM. I saw a figure in my bedroom. It was transparent and foggy, like it really wasn't there. I thought that it was just a figment of my sleep-deprived imagination.

The two of us stared at each other for about an hour, like we were expecting one or the other to leave. Soon after, this figure approached me. Before they vanished for the night, the figure whispered one thing.

"Remember us."

The next morning, that figure was standing in my living room, like it owed me non-existent rent. Naturally, I started asking it questions. Like, lots and lots of questions.

"What the hell are you doing here", "What do you want from me", "Who are you", y'know, what you'd ask a mysterious figure that may have broken into your house. My brother walked downstairs from his room, and saw me bombarding a ghost with questions. Understandably, he was very confused.

I tried explaining to him that this figure had broken into my room last night and I was now trying to extract questions from it. But it seemed like that answer confused him even more.

"Who the hell are you talking to?"

Those were the next words out of his mouth. And my indicator that there was something deeper going on.

Later in the day, I asked Karasu about this. He's one of the several magic residents, but it seemed like his specific kind of knowledge would help me more. I know that Andy's also magic, and no offense, but I can't see how his specific magic knowledge could help. Andy's is more internal and specific, Karasu's is more broad.

He took a long look at my pet ghost (yes, I have a pet ghost) and asked me if I had tried to listen to what it's saying to me. Of course, I hadn't.

I couldn't make out one voice. Rather, it was a collection of voices. They were all pleading me one thing. To remember them. To figure out what happened to them, so they can move on.

I stopped listening afterwards. It was giving me a headache.

"Why can everyone see my pet ghost?"

A reasonable question. The following are Karasu's words almost verbatim.

"Well, I guess that ghost is an amalgamation of several ghosts, which increases more ghost per ghost. The amount of ghostly energy coming from it is enough to make it visible to someone who isn't as sensitive to them as you."

Did that make sense? Hopefully it does. I somewhat understand it, but I also don't either.

Recap

Written by Admin K

A mysterious casette tape appeared in Souta's house, and we approached Andrew to listen to it.

There, we found out that one of the islanders (Aeris Tashita) had previously lived in an island known as "Rooster" Island, which had apparently destroyed. When we asked her about this, she said that she didn't remember living on such an island. Thus, we took it upon ourselves to learn more about this mysterious island!

Overtime, we've found more evidence and more tapes. We needed some place to keep track of all our discoveries, so we created this website! We write blog posts about interesting stuff that we find that relates to the mystery, or even stuff about ourselves. Hope you enjoy our quirky adventures on our blog!
